Privacy Policy

Effective: 1 October 2026

1. Information we collect

When you browse, place an order, request support, submit a return request or use store features, we may collect information such as your name, phone number, email (when provided), delivery address, city, PIN code, order contents, order/payment references, support messages and basic website analytics.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and fulfil orders, process or verify payments, provide tracking and support, handle returns/exchanges, prevent misuse or fraud, maintain inventory, understand store performance and improve the shopping experience.

3. Payments

Online payment checkout is handled by Dodo Payments or another payment provider shown at checkout. Payment-card or UPI credentials entered on the provider's secure payment page are handled by that provider; KODO's storefront does not intentionally store full card credentials.

4. Service providers & analytics

We may share information with providers needed to operate the store, such as payment, hosting, analytics, advertising, communication and shipping/fulfilment services. The site may use Google and Meta measurement tools; those providers may process device, browser and interaction data under their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Browser storage

The storefront uses browser storage for functions such as your cart, wishlist and order history on the device used for checkout. Clearing browser/site data can remove those local copies. Server-side merchant records, where applicable, are separate from your browser storage.

6. Retention & security

We retain information only as reasonably needed for order fulfilment, support, accounting, fraud prevention, legal obligations and legitimate business operations. We use technical and organisational measures intended to reduce unauthorised access, but no internet system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices

You may contact us to ask about, correct or request deletion of personal information, subject to records we must retain for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention or transaction purposes. You can also clear local browser data to remove locally stored cart/order-history information.

8. Policy updates & contact

We may update this policy when store features or service providers change. For privacy or order questions, email kododiy@gmail.com or contact KODO support on WhatsApp.
