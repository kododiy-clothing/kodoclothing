SHIPPING & DELIVERY TIMELINES

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Free Shipping Threshold

All prepaid orders above ₹799 qualify for 100% Free Express Air Shipping across India. Cash on Delivery (COD) orders incur a nominal ₹75 verification convenience charge.

2. Dispatch & Delivery Timelines

  • Standard Catalog Drops: Dispatched within 24 hours from our Delhi or Surat atelier.
  • DIY Custom Studio Prints: Dispatched within 36–48 hours after high-density 3D puff curing.
  • Delivery ETA: Metro cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad): 2–3 business days. Rest of India: 3–5 business days.

3. Live Courier Tracking & Notifications

As soon as your shipment is scanned by our primary courier partners (BlueDart, Delhivery, Xpressbees), a real-time Air Waybill (AWB) number is sent via WhatsApp and SMS, which can be tracked live at kodo.diy/track.
