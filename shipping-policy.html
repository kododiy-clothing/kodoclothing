Shipping & Delivery

Updated: 1 October 2026

1. Shipping charges

Orders with a merchandise subtotal of ₹799 or more qualify for free shipping under the standard checkout rules. Orders below the threshold are currently charged ₹99 unless a valid free-shipping promotion applies. The final shipping amount shown at checkout controls the order.

2. Processing & dispatch

Orders move to fulfilment after payment/advance and stock are confirmed. Standard and customised items can have different processing times. If an item requires additional production time or becomes unavailable, KODO may contact you before dispatch.

3. Delivery estimates

A typical delivery target after dispatch is approximately 2–5 business days within India. Remote locations, peak periods, weather, courier constraints and address issues can take longer. Estimates shown on the site are not guaranteed delivery dates.

4. Tracking

When a courier and AWB/tracking number are assigned, they can be shown in the Track Order experience or communicated by support. Tracking is not fabricated before courier assignment.

5. COD with advance

If COD with advance is available at checkout, the displayed advance is paid online first and the remaining eligible balance is collected on delivery. Availability can depend on order value, serviceability and operational checks.

6. Address issues, delays & support

Please enter a complete delivery address and reachable phone number. Contact KODO quickly if an address correction is needed; changes may not be possible after dispatch. For a delayed or missing shipment, email kododiy@gmail.com or contact WhatsApp support.
