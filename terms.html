CUSTOMER CHARTER

TERMS OF SERVICE & RETURNS

Effective Date: September 2026

1. 7-Day Hassle-Free Returns & Size Exchanges

Customers can initiate a size exchange or return within 7 calendar days of physical delivery via our online Returns Portal. Garments must be unworn, unwashed, and returned with original tags intact.

2. 3D Puff Print Quality Guarantee

All our 3D puff and high-density screen inks are cured at 165°C and guaranteed against peeling or cracking for up to 50 machine washes when washed inside-out in cold water.

3. Custom DIY Order Terms

Custom tees designed in the KODO.DIY studio are made to order. Because of personalization, returns on DIY pieces are accepted solely in the event of manufacturing defects, sizing discrepancies, or transit damage.
